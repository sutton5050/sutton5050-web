Costumier

Milly Whitefield

I'm a seamstress with a deep love of historical garments and striking costumes — happiest in the studio, creating a costume from the inside out and finishing it with detail.

After graduating with a first class Costume & Performance Design degree from Arts University Bournemouth, I spent two and a half years as a women's costumier with the Royal Shakespeare Company, and now work freelance, making for theatre, television and film — taking costumes from pattern to finished piece.

I'm constantly evolving to grow my creative skillset through workshops and courses — including upholstery, silversmithing and pottery — and I'm always wanting to expand my knowledge.
